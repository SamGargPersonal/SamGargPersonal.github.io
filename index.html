Featured Project

OmniTax Professionals Website

Client Work · Tax Advisory · Brisbane, Australia

A complete static rebuild for a Queensland tax advisory practice — from CMS migration through publishing tools the client can run themselves.

The challenge

The firm needed a credible, fast site that could showcase senior “Big 4” experience, publish fortnightly Tax News PDFs, present a growing team, and meet Australian privacy and terms requirements — without WordPress fees, plugin risk, or ongoing developer dependency.

What I delivered

  • Full site architecture — clean directory URLs across 7+ public pages, fixed navigation, and responsive layouts from hero through footer
  • Custom PDF experience — reusable PDF.js viewer with page previews on desktop and a mobile dock, used for Tax News, team bios, and legal documents
  • Insights publishing system — manifest-driven grid with date sorting, retina-sharp thumbnails, and GitHub Actions workflows to add reports without editing HTML
  • Team management — JSON-driven Our People page with configurable photo cropping, leadership section, and the same admin workflow for new hires
  • Legal & compliance — Privacy Policy and Terms of Use aligned with the Australian Privacy Act, with inline PDF viewing
  • Brand polish — TPB and CAANZ registration badges, refined typography, client values content, and a private client portal kept off public navigation

Result: A professional, maintainable site the firm owns outright — fast, secure, and free to host, with workflows that let them update content themselves.
